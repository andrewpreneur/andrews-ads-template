# Creative material
Claude builds ads from these. Real photos of real people beat stock and AI images.
- `photos/`: headshot, 20+ natural photos and reaction shots of the face of the brand, cut-outs with no background (PNG).
- `logos/`: transparent PNGs, light and dark versions.
- `videos/`: past ads, testimonials, talking-to-camera clips. Large files are better in Google Drive (share the folder
  and put its link in `client/config.json` → `links.secondBrainFolder`). If a video already runs as an ad, reuse that post
  instead of uploading it again.
- `winning-ads/`: screenshots of ads that worked, with what they cost per result if you know it.
- `testimonials/`: reviews, results and screenshots you're OK using in ads.
Don't put customer lists or personal data here.
